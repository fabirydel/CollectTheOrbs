Collect The Orbs: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Collect The Orbs ("the game") is a mobile game by Fabian Rydel ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game and its third-party services handle.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (scores, unlocked ships and galaxies, upgrades, orb counts and settings) in a local database on your device. This data stays on your device and is not sent to me. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Information I collect

I do not run any servers for the game and I do not collect, store or sell personal information myself. The game does not ask you to create an account.

Advertising

The free version of the game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and information about how you interact with ads, in order to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings. See Google's advertising policy and privacy policy.

A one-time in-app purchase removes ads.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. I do not receive or store your payment details; I only learn that the purchase was made so the ads can be removed.

Google Play Games

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, your score and achievements are sent to Google Play Games Services and are handled under Google's privacy policy. Signing in is optional.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
